Source: [[docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-08-feat017-closure-slice-design#Semantic challenge review|FEAT-017 coordinated closure slice]].

> Status: proposed; semantic adoption remains subject to the existing per-SR human consent workflow.

The deterministic keyword tripwire may remain as a low-cost supplementary detector, but it is not
semantic challenge review. A host may obtain review through its available agent mechanism, yet the
persisted challenge schema and human-decision boundary are Coherence-owned and host-neutral.
