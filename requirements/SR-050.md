The intent artifact is the durable source for the user's words; a later spec may summarize them, but may not substitute for them.

The mature workflow may extend the artifact with resumable capture metadata while preserving schema-one reads.

> Status: proposed; semantic adoption remains subject to the existing human consent workflow.
