Source: [[docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-04-completion-gate-duplication-seed|Two completion-gate
authorities must not silently diverge — seed]].

Discovered while scoping [[SR-050]] T3 (the implementation-workflow relation obligation):
`factory.preflight.checks.run_completion_preflight` is the function actually wired into the live
orchestrator (`factory/orchestrator/runner.py`) — its `BLOCKING` issues are what really flips a
run's outcome to `"escalated"` and sends a task back to `todo`. It has never consulted
`coherence.policy.compiler.compile_obligations`, the durable Obligation model every other SR-050
step (T1/T4/T5) and [[SR-059]] built against. Both already share the same underlying objects
(`substrate.ledger.tasks.Task`, `coherence.register.register`'s requirements, `coherence.trace`'s
graph — confirmed directly: `factory.trace.graph`/`factory.orchestrator.ledger`/
`factory.requirements.register` are deprecated re-exports of their `coherence`/`substrate`
equivalents), but the completion-check logic itself is hand-rolled twice, against two different
data sources: the live gate reads this run's own not-yet-persisted `transcript_dir` output; the
durable model reads the evidence store already committed to `evidence/runs/`.

**Deliberately not designed yet.** [[SR-050]] T3 resolves this narrowly for its own new check —
the relation-maintenance obligation has no legacy duplicate to reconcile, so it is built directly
as a `coherence.policy.compiler` Obligation and wired into the live gate from the start. It
deliberately leaves the pre-existing `validation_missing`/`validation_failed`/`validation_stale`/
`review_missing`/`must_fix_unresolved` checks untouched, because naively pointing them at
`compile_obligations` today would make them blind to the run currently in flight (see the seed
doc's timing argument). This SR exists so that corpus-wide unification question has something
tracking it on disk, rather than being left as an artifact of how far T3 happened to reach. No
acceptance criteria are authored here.
