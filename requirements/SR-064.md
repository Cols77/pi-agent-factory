Source: [[docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-04-coherence-tooling-performance-seed|Coherence tooling
must stay usable as the corpus grows — seed]].

Raised directly by the user while scoping [[SR-050]] T3: every FEAT-001 review mechanism built so
far (`coherence register review`, `--fidelity`, `overlap-check`, and T3's own new per-task
reconciliation pass) is correct against today's corpus size, but none has a stated performance
budget or has been measured against a larger one. `coherence.register.overlap` ([[SR-058]]) is the
one place this was already addressed proactively, with a content-fingerprint cache built in from
the start specifically because naive TF-IDF-over-everything was recognized as a scaling risk at
design time — the rest of the register/trace/policy read paths re-parse and re-derive their inputs
on most calls, with no stated ceiling.

**Deliberately not designed yet.** No benchmark harness, fixture corpora, or budget numbers are
committed here — optimizing before measuring risks solving the wrong bottleneck. This SR exists so
the concern has something tracking it on disk before it becomes a real, felt problem, rather than
being addressed only in reaction to it. No acceptance criteria are authored here.
