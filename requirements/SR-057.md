Source: [[docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-22-coherence-progressive-assurance-design.md#4|Coherence: progressive assurance, obligations and lifecycle relationships]], the "Typed lifecycle relationships" bullet.

Split off from [[SR-011]] during its human authoring consent (2026-09-03). SR-011's original draft
statement bundled two claims together: typed task justification (implemented, tested, now SR-011's
whole scope) and this broader typed lifecycle-relationship vocabulary applied to non-task node
kinds. `coherence.trace.model.EdgeKind` declares all twelve relationship-kind names as a
type-level `Literal`, but nothing parses a `relationships:`-style frontmatter field on any non-task
node into such an edge, and there is no load-time rejection path for an unsupported kind analogous
to task justification's `InvalidJustificationError` — grepped as zero occurrences outside the
`Literal` declaration itself.

**Deliberately not designed yet.** D16 (design doc §3 decisions table) frames this wider vocabulary
as schema-only, deferred until a real use case needs it — the same posture as the seven-dimension
profile vocabulary ([[SR-008]]'s own gap). No acceptance criteria are authored here. This SR exists
so the design doc's broader claim has something tracking it on disk, rather than being quietly
dropped when SR-011 narrowed to what it could actually prove.
