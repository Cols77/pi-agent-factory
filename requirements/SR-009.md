**Statement corrected during authoring consent (2026-09-03).** It originally listed "status,
health, inbox, navigator, and gates" as the consumers of the compiled `Obligation` contract,
quoting the source design doc's §4 verbatim. Grepping `src/coherence` for
`compile_obligations`/`Obligation` usage confirms three of the five: `coherence.navigate.health`
(dimensions 4/5 and 11), `coherence.navigate.obligations` (the navigator's effective-profile
view, wired into `coherence.navigate.cli`), and `coherence.policy.ci` (`required_ci_commands`).
The other two do not exist in the current code: `status_snapshot` (`src/coherence/status.py`) and
`coherence register status` (`src/coherence/register/cli.py`) have zero references to obligations
or the compiler, and `list_items` (`src/coherence/inbox.py`) likewise never consumes a compiled
obligation. Dropped "status" and "inbox" from the statement rather than keep an unbacked claim —
no acceptance criterion referenced either, so none needed changing beyond the statement text
itself.

Wiring `status.py`'s probes and `inbox.py`'s `list_items` to the compiled obligation set is a real
design decision (which obligations surface in a status line or inbox item, at what requiredness
threshold, for which scope) that no increment in the design doc's own integration map (§10)
assigns: Increment 2C covers only CI/gates, 3B only the navigator, 5 only the health vector. That
remains open follow-on work for a future SR, not something this authoring pass can close by
drafting an acceptance criterion no test could honestly back today.
