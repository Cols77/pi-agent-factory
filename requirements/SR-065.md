This requirement directly adds the user-facing host orchestration obligation. It consumes existing
planning-pipeline, consent, persistence, cross-artifact review, and gate-pack contracts; it neither
redefines their authority nor turns Pi, Hermes, or Kanban transport state into an approval path.
