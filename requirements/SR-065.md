Source: [[docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-08-feat017-closure-slice-design#lifecycle-contract|FEAT-017 coordinated closure slice]].

> Status: proposed; semantic adoption remains subject to the existing per-SR human consent workflow.

This requirement directly adds the user-facing host orchestration obligation. It consumes existing
planning-pipeline, consent, persistence, cross-artifact review, and gate-pack contracts; it neither
redefines their authority nor turns Pi, Hermes, or Kanban transport state into an approval path.

Existing host command-and-hook workflows may remain as explicit compatibility paths. They retain
their own adapter and hook protections but do not claim a lifecycle-derived stage; this requirement
requires that the guided entrypoint make manual sequencing unnecessary, not that a user lose an
existing manual path during migration.
