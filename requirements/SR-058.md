Source: [[docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-03-cross-feat-semantic-overlap-detection-seed|Cross-FEAT/SR semantic overlap detection at scale — seed]].

Discovered during [[SR-004]]'s authoring-consent decision (2026-09-03), alongside [[SR-057]]:
having found that SR-050 reuses [[SR-023]] and extends [[SR-049]] with no typed relation recording
either link, the human asked the broader question — at hundreds or thousands of SRs and FEATs, how
do you reliably catch overlap **nobody wrote down anywhere**, where neither requirement's author
knew about the other and so no prose mention exists for a mirror check to find? That is a
different detection problem from [[SR-057]]'s (which finds gaps between what was declared and what
was narrated): this one finds overlap between what was written and other things also written, with
no declared or narrated link between them at all. Kept as a separate SR from [[SR-057]]
deliberately — [[SR-057]]'s mechanism is deterministic and `test_marker`-able; this one is
necessarily similarity-based and needs a judgement call on every candidate, never a bare pass/fail.

**Deliberately not designed yet.** No acceptance criteria are authored here. The seed doc above
records technical recon toward the eventual design — a two-layer shape (deterministic structural
completeness, which is [[SR-057]]'s territory, plus embedding-based candidate generation narrowed
by an intersection with SR-057's declared-relation data, verified by a model only on the surviving
candidates, never auto-applied) and an explicit "not required, SQLite if anything, never a second
system of record" answer to the storage/tech-stack question — but the real design, including
where the similarity threshold sits and exactly how a finding surfaces, waits for someone to plan
it against real corpus scale. Must integrate with [[FEAT-017]] PLANNING-BOOTSTRAP's authoring
workflow per the human's explicit requirement, so a plausible duplicate is flagged while a new SR
or FEAT is being drafted, not only found later in an audit.
