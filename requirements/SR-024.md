**Relations declared 2026-09-05, on first change under [[SR-050]]/AC-5.** This requirement owns
"record verdicts" — so the code deciding *whether a verdict may be durably recorded at all* is its
implementation, not merely adjacent to it. `cmd_validate` is the only place a canonical validation
report is produced from a real sweep and the only place entitled to stamp `recorded_by: "harness"`;
`report_has_measurement` and `existing_report_origin` are the two predicates that decide whether
this run's verdicts may replace what is already on disk.

Declared because SR-050/AC-5's completion gate requires changed production code to be reachable
from a satisfying requirement's relations, and this requirement had none. The declaration is
deliberately narrow: the harness implementations themselves (`sim_harness.py`,
`playwright_harness.py`) and the evidence-manifest readers are *also* this requirement's territory
by its statement, but naming them here without having changed or verified them would be the
overstatement [[SR-050]]/AC-4's fidelity review exists to catch.
