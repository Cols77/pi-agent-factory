# FEAT-016 — MODULAR-WORKFLOWS

Status: declared feature dossier (Inc-9 health-resolution, decision-level register).

This feature registers **MODULAR-WORKFLOWS** in the Coherence / pi-agent-factory feature set. It covers declarative workflows, an interpreter, standard/templates, workflow-specific assurance floors, controlled extensions, and host-neutral GoalSpec stages.


## Design boundary

A workflow declares the stages, transitions, gate bindings, goal-loop behavior, evidence
requirements, and exhaustion outcomes for a run. Coherence compiles the workflow together
with project policy and change scope into an immutable contract and GatePlan before
execution.

The workflow model has two layers:

- Global invariants: contract identity/hash, scope protection, evidence provenance,
  fail-closed unknown/missing states, control-plane integrity, and human-boundary handling.
- Workflow-specific assurance floors: the minimum gates that this workflow cannot bypass.

Flexible stages and project-local gates may add assurance around that floor. An agent may
propose a stronger workflow, but may not reduce required assurance. A reduction or revision
of definitions, thresholds, applicability, protected tests, or floors goes through the
human-owned change-control workflow and takes effect only in a later contract revision.

GoalSpec is host-neutral and includes an objective, evaluator, required evidence, budget,
and exhaustion outcome. Hermes Kanban goal-mode is an execution optimization for a bounded
stage; its continuation/evaluator result is an observation consumed by Coherence, not final
authority. Kanban cards are materialized by the selected transport and do not define the
workflow semantics.
